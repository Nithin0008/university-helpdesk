SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY AND ELIGIBILITY FOR SEMESTER EXAMS
1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement:
Every enrolled student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered theory course and 80% in practical laboratory sessions to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.

1.2 Condonation of Attendance Shortage:
Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for medical condonation by submitting certified medical documents from an authorized hospital within 7 working days of returning to campus. A medical condonation fee of INR 1,000 per course applies upon approval by the Dean of Academic Affairs.

1.3 Attendance Below 65%:
Students with attendance below 65% in any course will receive an 'FA' (Failed due to Attendance) grade and must re-register for the course in the supplementary or summer semester.


SECTION 2: HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION AND RESIDENTIAL RULES
2.1 Hostel Curfew and Night Entry Timings (రాత్రి కర్ఫ్యూ / ਰਾਤ ਦਾ ਕਰਫਿਊ):
Hostel entry gates are strictly closed for night curfew at 9:30 PM on weekdays (Monday through Friday) and at 10:30 PM on weekends (Saturday and Sunday). All residential students must return to the hostel premises before night curfew hours. Late entry without prior permission from the Chief Warden will result in a warning letter on the first offense and disciplinary action for repeated violations.

2.2 Night Out and Leave Requests:
Students planning to stay outside overnight must submit an online e-leave application on the Student Portal at least 24 hours in advance. Parental verification via OTP or registered phone call is mandatory before approval.

2.3 Hostel Mess, Canteen, and Dining Timings:
The hostel mess and dining hall serves meals according to the following schedule:
- Breakfast Service: 7:30 AM to 9:15 AM
- Lunch Service: 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM
- Evening Snacks: 4:45 PM to 6:00 PM
- Dinner Service: 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM
Special dietary requests or sick-meal trays can be requested through the hostel mess warden.


SECTION 3: SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
3.1 Merit Scholarship:
Students ranking in the top 5% of their academic batch with a CGPA of 9.0 or higher receive a 50% tuition fee waiver for the subsequent academic semester.

3.2 Merit-cum-Means Financial Aid:
Financial assistance covering up to 40% of tuition fees is available for students whose combined family annual income does not exceed INR 5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees). Applications open in the first month of every semester. Income tax returns and municipal certificates are required as proof.

3.3 Sports and Cultural Excellence Scholarships:
State, national, and international level sports medalists are eligible for up to a 100% sports scholarship including hostel fee concessions. Applications must be submitted to the Directorate of Physical Education.


SECTION 4: EXAMINATION, GRADING SYSTEM, AND RE-EVALUATION
4.1 Grading Scale:
The university follows a 10-point relative grading scale:
- O (Outstanding): Grade Point 10 (90% and above)
- A+ (Excellent): Grade Point 9 (80% to 89%)
- A (Very Good): Grade Point 8 (70% to 79%)
- B+ (Good): Grade Point 7 (60% to 69%)
- B (Above Average): Grade Point 6 (50% to 59%)
- C (Pass): Grade Point 5 (40% to 49%)
- F (Fail): Below 40%

4.2 Paper Re-evaluation and Answer Script Inspection:
Students who wish to have their end-semester examination papers re-evaluated may apply within 15 days of result declaration through the examination portal. The re-evaluation fee is INR 500 per paper. If marks increase by more than 10%, 50% of the re-evaluation fee is refunded.
