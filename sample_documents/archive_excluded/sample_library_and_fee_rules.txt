================================================================================
APEX GLOBAL UNIVERSITY - LIBRARY REGULATIONS & TUITION FEE TIMELINES
Academic Year 2026-2027
================================================================================

SECTION 1: CENTRAL LIBRARY BORROWING REGULATIONS
1.1 Book Issue Limits:
- Undergraduate Students: 4 books for 14 calendar days.
- Postgraduate Students: 6 books for 21 calendar days.
- Research Scholars: 10 books for 30 calendar days.

1.2 Overdue Fines and Late Returns (ਕਿਤਾਬਾਂ 'ਤੇ ਦੇਰੀ ਜੁਰਮਾਨਾ / లేట్ ఫైన్):
A fine of INR 5 per day per book is levied for the first 7 days of delay beyond the due date. From day 8 onwards, the overdue fine increases to INR 15 per day per book. Damaged or lost books must be replaced with the newest edition or reimbursed at twice the market value.

1.3 Digital Library and E-Resources Access:
All registered students have 24/7 access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, JSTOR, and Springer journals using their university email ID and institutional SSO login.


SECTION 2: TUITION FEE PAYMENT DEADLINES AND LATE PAYMENT PENALTIES
2.1 Payment Schedule:
Semester tuition fees must be cleared on or before the following cut-off dates:
- Monsoon/Fall Semester: 25th July 2026
- Spring Semester: 10th January 2027

2.2 Late Fee Structure:
- 1 to 10 days delay: Flat late fee of INR 1,000.
- 11 to 25 days delay: Flat late fee of INR 2,500.
- Exceeding 25 days delay: The student's portal access is temporarily frozen and course registration is put on hold until dues are cleared.

2.3 Online Payment Modes:
Payments must be submitted exclusively via the student ERP portal using UPI, Net Banking, or Debit/Credit card. Cash payments are not accepted at the accounts office.
